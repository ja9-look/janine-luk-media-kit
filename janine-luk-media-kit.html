<about />

Hi, I'm Janine.

I'm a London-based creator building a community around the idea that ambition and aesthetics aren't mutually exclusive. By day, I work in STEM; outside of it, I make content on motivation, fitness, and the modern multi-hyphenate girl's lifestyle.

My audience is made up of driven, career-focused women who care deeply about how they present themselves — and who trust me for honest, thoughtful recommendations that fit their fast-paced, multifaceted lives.

I partner with brands whose products actually fit into the world I'm building: pieces that look as good in a 9am meeting as they do at a 7pm event, tools that make daily life easier, and brands that respect the intelligence of their audience.
