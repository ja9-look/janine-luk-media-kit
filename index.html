<about />

Let me introduce myself.

I'm a London-based software engineer and content creator building a community around the idea that ambition, fitness, and a good work life balance aren't mutually exclusive. By day, I work as a Senior Software Engineer building Data and AI platforms for Google's customers. Outside of that, I make content on career tips, motivation, fitness, golf, and the modern multi-hyphenate women's lifestyle.

My audience is made up of driven, career-focused women who care deeply about how they present themselves - and who trust me for honest, thoughtful recommendations that fit their fast-paced, multi-faceted lives.

I partner with brands whose products genuinely fit into the world I'm building - pieces that look as good in a 9am meeting as they do at a 7pm event, tools that make daily life easier, and brands that respect the intelligence of their audience.
